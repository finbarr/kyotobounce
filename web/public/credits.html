Credits

Kyoto Bounce is an independently developed game. It is not affiliated with Kyoto Station, its operators, or its architect.

Station geometry

Parts of the station geometry are adapted from the Kyoto City 3D city model, provided by Kyoto City under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Changes include coordinate registration, selection and removal of geometry, reconstructed public spaces, revised materials, collision geometry, and game-specific details. Kyoto City's source is not an endorsement of this game.

Additional modeled details, robot art, procedural textures, code, and synthesized audio are created by Kyoto Bounce contributors. Project-created work is MIT licensed; the adapted municipal geometry retains its CC BY 4.0 attribution.

Research and software

Public photographs, station maps, and architectural research informed the reconstruction. Research photographs are not shipped as game textures. Source notes and software are in the Kyoto Bounce repository. Three.js and other dependencies retain their respective licenses; the Unity runtime is proprietary.
