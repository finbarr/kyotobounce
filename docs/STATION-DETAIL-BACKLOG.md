## Completion evidence

- 44 named reference views, approach and reverse-angle captures, plus three evening installation views.
- 5,276 generated objects, 162,848 added triangles, 3,074 matched physical solids and 137 sign faces.
- 20/20 native walking routes, 430 floor-support samples and 12/12 native ball-contact checks.
- 30/30 courses, each with two full-clear native solutions at physical rest; 26/35 neighboring shots also full-clear, all 35 settle normally.
- Type checking, unit tests, full lossless asset verification and isolated service integration. Matched render/download results are in the implementation report.

Maps remain schematic, some photo dates are unknown, and store interiors are original interpretations. No research photographs are distributed as textures. Use the current bundle and [asset workflow](ASSETS.md); do not keep a second playable scene or historical course catalog.
