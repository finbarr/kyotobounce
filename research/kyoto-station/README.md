## What the comparison says

The game's major public-space identities and landmarks are already represented. The strongest upgrades are credible material/light response, better facade and structural proportions, fuller planting, and the northern/southern streets and plazas. The [gap analysis](GAP-ANALYSIS.md) separates represented features from incomplete areas and unresolved coverage questions.

The plan assumes **the station, both plazas and immediately surrounding street frontages**, with distant city blocks as visible context. It deliberately bounds completion with 12 daylight and three evening reference views, runtime checks and performance gates on this laptop in Chrome. These are proposed targets for future work, not claims that the current release meets them.

## Organization and reuse

- `sources.json` — authoritative machine-readable register: original URLs, publisher, dates, notes, retrieval result, local paths, inspection level, rights and hashes.
- `SOURCES.md` — readable full catalogue.
- `maps/`, `plans/`, `architecture/`, `photos/`, `context/` — downloaded references and selected PDF previews.
- `videos/README.md`, `panoramas/README.md` — external viewer links and coverage notes.
- `current-model/` — current captures and exact baseline inventory; not photograph-matched comparisons.
- `notes/validation.json` — local-file/hash verification and counts.
- `scripts/` — collection and index-generation helpers. Run from the repository root; review the catalogue before fetching again.

Reference media stays outside Git and the shipped asset pack. Saved photographs and drawings are research references, not licensed game textures. Preserve source-specific attribution and obtain suitable permissions or create original assets before distribution. This directory is intentionally ignored by the repository; keep a separate backup if moving or deleting the checkout.

The initial research collection changed no game code, scene geometry, production service or scoring data. The subsequent user-set reconstruction goal is active in an isolated worktree; its generated candidates and verification records remain local.
