# Restoring the north facade's inner returns

[Actual-game comparison](frontage-seams/index.html)

The first facade import clipped through both inner return walls. Four exact sightlines hit the old broad western pier in the game and a different, nearer wall in the registered municipal source. The repair extends the imported source to include the complete returns and removes the superseded west pier from both rendering and native collision. The actual Chrome views confirm that the projecting block and exposed inner cavities are gone.

Candidate 02 contains 433 restored meshes / 109,214 native triangles. Compared with candidate 01, the browser adds 4,836 triangles, four batches and 220,208 delivery bytes. These source surfaces remain a coarse architectural baseline; texture and junction quality still need work.

## Gameplay and circulation

Removing the old pier changes one final bank in **Sculpture Square Slingshot**. Source rays at the former contact region show an open lower passage, supporting removal of the artificial obstruction. The candidate keeps all six waypoints and its launch hint, destination radius and scoring. Its destination moves about 14.4 m west and 1.7 m north to the resulting landing on the concourse; the course revision increments from 2 to 3. Its measured route length is 190.8 m. Native floor checks and an actual out-and-back capsule walk validate that approach.
