# Corrected gate facade and the missing rear passage

28 September 2026. [Before/after gallery and evidence](central-gate-facade/index.html).

## Construction correction

The earlier uninstalled bay placed the dark stone boundary at Y=3.7m. Exact municipal vertices 451/454 instead place this boundary at Y=5.751988m. The new sample follows that source boundary and adds both four-column, two-row groups of square recesses beside the opening.

All 64 recess corner coordinates were checked against `Untitled.200`. Their X/Y bounds are retained; projection onto the existing slightly sloping facade plane differs from the source Z by at most 7.28mm. The 37mm blind depth is an explicit construction assumption. Face slabs are cut around each opening; the continuous backing closes the recess. These are modeled recesses, not dark decals covering solid front tiles.

The corrected study has 649 records, 10,700 triangles, eight material batches and nine existing procedural textures. Browser/native positions agree within 1.04 micrometres, winding agrees, all meshes are closed and all face normals pass. Eighty independent rays through the sixteen openings hit their recessed backs at the expected depth. All 397 records outside the gate-side/return scope preserve their geometry. Two historical `floor` material names become the already-established `granite`/`stone` keys; this is explicitly recorded rather than hidden in the scope proof.

The before/after and close-up views use fixed Chrome cameras and studio lighting. They show a construction change, not accepted station lighting or a photographic fit. The study retains bay07's older floor only for comparison. **Do not install it over the complete newer basement assembly.** Full station candidate05 and active game assets are unchanged.

## What the city mesh does and does not model

A head-on source render shows the recessed bridge, departure boards and gate line painted into a flat facade texture. They are not separate volumetric bridge geometry.

An exact box/triangle audit tested all 303,303 source triangles in the region X=-7.076…9.923m, Y=0.3…10.715m and Z=6.449…35m. Only two triangles intersect: `Untitled.203` faces 7601/7602, forming a broad rear envelope plane. That plane is 23.706–23.732m behind the gate frontage over the opening width. It does not establish the gate equipment line, bridge floor height or interior wall clearance.

This qualifies the earlier vertex-only inspection: there is a rear envelope, but no intervening bridge in this audit volume. The current 5.6m returns describe a partial construction sample. Do not mistake them for a measured complete hall depth, or automatically move the existing roughly 22m-recessed equipment forward.

## Current operator circulation evidence

The [hotel's official floor guide](https://jrwest-hotels.jp/granvia-kyoto/floor/) and July 2026 [floor map](https://jrwest-hotels.jp/granvia-kyoto/files/pdf/floormap.pdf), PDF page3, show an M3F corridor between the western north–south free passage and an eastern descent to the 2F hotel lobby. Its narrow glazed connection provides a useful candidate identity for the bridge visible above the gates. **Matching that photographed bridge to this map is still an inference.** The map has north pointing down; the station's 2F overview has north up.

The operator diagrams establish route adjacency and floor names, not measured span, depth or elevation. The 1997 World Architecture sections provide building context but do not resolve the bridge dimensions at the available scale. No new bridge volume is claimed or inserted in this revision.
