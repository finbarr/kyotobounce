# Additional historical operator account

[Urban Advance 12, December 1998](https://www.nup.or.jp/nui/user/media/document/information/urbanadvance/h10/12.pdf), by Shigeyuki Uchida of Kyoto Station Building Development. [Saved PDF](../architecture/urban-advance-12.pdf).

Visually inspected the full article: PDF pages 69-74, printed pages 67-72.

- PDF p69 lists a 59.8 m height, 470 m east-west length and 60-80 m north-south width. These are broad building dimensions, not coordinates for individual floors.
- PDF p70 describes 171 Grand Staircase steps and three Skyway observation points, corroborating other references.
- PDF p71 has a labeled perspective showing the public spaces and connecting routes.
- PDF p73 describes 73 stone varieties selected from 288 samples, useful evidence against treating the station as one uniform stone material.

Use this historical account for durable architecture. Current tenant names, furniture and landscaping need modern references. The perspective is schematic and cannot establish exact floor heights or stair slopes.
