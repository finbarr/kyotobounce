# Basement routes and current retail references

Research update: 28 September 2026. This resolves the first-landing identity left open in the earlier basement study. It adds references and planning constraints; it does not change the game.

## Route identity

The [official Porta route guide](https://www.porta.co.jp/navigation) explicitly describes: central gate → first escalator to **B1F** → turn back beside Ogaki Bookstore → second escalator to **B2F South**. The saved [six photographs](../photos/porta-b2-route-01.jpg) follow this sequence. The station's shorter directions describe the final B2F destination without spelling out the intermediate turn.

The first modeled descent therefore needs B1F shop/circulation connections. A convincing basement cannot stop at a closed lower landing. The evidence establishes route sequence, not a metric B2F elevation.

## Current plans and historical changes

The [five-page Porta tenant guide](../maps/porta-shopguide-2026.pdf) supplies the B2F plan and a schematic section on PDF page 2; station B1F is on page 3. Page 1 bears a 2026.5.29 date, while PDF metadata records a July 2026 creation date. Treat those as separate document clues. Station B1F and the plaza's Porta B1F must not be assumed to share one vertical datum simply because their floor names match.

The [20 February 2025 operator announcement](https://jrwsc.co.jp/storage/files/porta.pdf), saved [locally](../plans/porta-renovation-2025.pdf), describes new B1F retail frontage under the central gate. Its first two pages show the renewal zone and three relocating craft shops: Kanoya Kitagishi, Yoshitoyo and Yasuda Nenju. The appearance illustrations are explicitly proposed CG renderings, not completion photographs. The 2026 tenant guide lists the shops, corroborating their later presence but not every illustrated finish.

## Missing-detail checklist for this area

1. Open B1F retail portals, circulation connections and visible shop depth.
2. Correct distinction between B1F and B2F surfaces and ceiling systems.
3. Blue upper lining, stone returns, vents, soffit joints and supported signs.
4. The second descent and its lower connections, after obtaining defensible geometry.
5. Current retail frontage references following the 2025 renovation.
6. Tactile-path continuity and threshold details without invented collision obstacles.

Before construction, align multiple views to stable architecture and record unknown dimensions. Source maps are schematic; neither a floor label nor a proposed rendering supplies survey accuracy. These findings refine G13, G17 and G18 in the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md).
