# Gate passage and bridge: connected construction with unresolved dimensions

28 September 2026. [Construction and reference comparison](central-gate-passage-bridge/index.html).

## What was built

The new component supplies a ground floor from the source portal to the municipal rear envelope, longer stone returns, a curved bridge body, a narrow glazed crossing, landing stubs through both sides, a ribbed ceiling, waist rails and simple lamps. All solid shapes have identical browser/native geometry. It joins the corrected facade study for inspection; it has **not** replaced the current hall/basement or active game assets.

The latest draft has 191 records, 42 colliding solids, 2,480 triangles and seven material batches. It uses no image textures. These are initial material values and studio views; the render lacks the station's lighting, reflections and surrounding geometry. The old sample floor in the paired views is not the current basement assembly.

## Evidence changed the bridge position

The first draft put the crossing near the rear envelope, with floor Y=7.35m and front depth20.7m. It was geometrically valid and traversable, but the unchanged July2026 hall camera hid the bridge behind a return wall. The photograph clearly shows glazing and fascia in that opening. This rejects the draft as a solution for that reference.

In the second draft, a provisional image point at (878,470) in a1200×800 viewport marks the apparent east end of the bridge glazing's lower edge. Intersecting that ray with the source east-wall plane, X=9.922836m, gives floor Y≈9.58m and depth≈6.68m behind the front wall. This makes the bridge visible in the appropriate part of the unchanged reference camera.

**This is a construction hypothesis, not metric registration.** The camera was fitted to upper architecture. The lower wall-plane identity and exact floor edge remain uncertain. Perturbing the image point by±24px yields floor Y8.46–10.72m and depth5.52–7.87m; these are sensitivity intervals, not validated confidence bounds. No geometry acceptance tolerance was widened. The complete-photo comparison remains unaccepted.

The bridge width follows the source opening, about17m. Its2.3m enclosure depth,1.5m curved fascia bow,1.25m fascia thickness,2.6m internal height,11 glass bays and ceiling step are explicit estimates. The ceiling rises behind the retained5.6m front sample to clear this proposed crossing. Neither the ceiling step nor the full hidden bridge plan has been established by a drawing.

## New architectural references

The [official hotel floor guide](https://jrwest-hotels.jp/granvia-kyoto/floor/) places the narrow M3F connection between the western free passage and the route to the eastern2F lobby. A [first-person March2024 account](https://iko-yo.net/experiences/88517) explicitly places the hotel corridor above the central gate, with railway views on the other side. This strengthens the bridge identity; it does not survey its elevation or establish present-day access rules.

The [travel account and photographs](https://chottoiitoko.com/kashikojimabasaratei-shimakaze/) show a straight enclosed crossing with full-height glazing, a glossy dark tiled floor, waist rails, pale ceiling ribs and small lamps. Its article describes January2025 while filenames indicate January2026; capture date is unresolved. Seasonal string lights were not added to the model. Photographs remain research references; no pixels are used in game assets.

Two images following those corridor photographs actually show high-level views over East Square and the Tower/forecourt. They were individually inspected and relabeled as exterior references. Their catalogue IDs retain their initial names so saved links remain stable. They must not be used as M3F camera evidence.

## Checks and limits

Browser/native serialization agrees within1.01 micrometres; triangle winding, closedness, positive volumes and geometric face normals pass. The isolated native receipt covers ground routes through the center and beside each wall, plus a bridge crossing through the end openings, in both directions. It does not prove a complete station route, course clearance or final appearance. The first two native harness attempts failed because open-sky results were mishandled; the corrected test compares ceiling hits with independent triangle rays and recognizes the worker's empty disk representation.

The broad municipal rear plane is still23.7m behind the source front. That does **not** locate ticket barriers or justify a constant-width walled tunnel all the way to the tracks. The current draft's continued side walls behind the bridge are not accepted: the reference shows a wider paid concourse and railway view beyond it. Gate equipment, departure boards and that widening still need coherent construction. Do not move the old equipment or install this draft based solely on its passing walking checks.
