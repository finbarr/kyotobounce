## What changed

The obsolete canopy is removed: 5,866 panels and 33,293 frame members. The old Skyway, its fixtures and access routes remain while the replacement's western restaurant approach and eastern court connections are resolved. This candidate is not ready for promotion.

- Four remaining interfaces involve the imported inner shell or eastern cladding. They need geometric inspection before deciding whether to trim either surface. Four other changed first hits are the newly installed truss in front of expectations from the old outer-only component.
- Thirty of the 31 authored shots pass and finish at full physical rest. Skyway Trapdoor cannot place its old waypoint because the roof surface it used has been removed. This failure remains open.
- The thirty executed shots retain duration, hits and destination results. Twenty-eight complete pose sequences are identical. Ceiling Service retains identical positions; Cafe Carom has a maximum position difference of 0.162 mm. The existing terrace destination revision and two passage walks are revalidated.

The complete isolated runtime suite passes, including recovery, scoring, the Grand Slam finale, replay/sharing, both playback speeds, designer validation and 64 rapid recalls. This does not override the failed Skyway course.

The two wall closures observed at the eastern entrance in the untrimmed source are absent from the installed route volumes: all 740 imported municipal station panels were checked. No imported wall was cut.
