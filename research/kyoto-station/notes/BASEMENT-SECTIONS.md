## Modeling decision

The [official route evidence](BASEMENT-ROUTE-UPDATE.md) establishes two successive descents: concourse → B1F turnback → B2F south. These new sources do **not** establish the metric drop of the second flight. The model's existing B1 elevation and municipal mesh minimum cannot be extrapolated into a surveyed B2 elevation.

Before building the lower connection:

1. Match the B1 turnback and second-flight approach in at least two photographs, using the first bank, fixed piers and existing floor edges as anchors.
2. Fit visible opening edges and lower-flight direction separately from floor height; record which dimensions remain assumptions.
3. Obtain a legible section or a calibrated view containing both second-flight landings before treating its rise as measured. If unavailable, keep any study explicitly provisional and out of the accepted station geometry.
4. Construct the complete floor opening, guards, return landings and B2 corridor as one connected assembly. A staircase inserted beneath the current solid floor would not create a usable route.
5. Validate visual/collision agreement, walking clearance, moving rides and affected courses before integration; the existing first-flight checks do not cover the new descent.

The missing dimensional evidence is local to this connection. Other station, plaza, material and lighting work in the long-term plan remains available.
