# Source and library recheck — 27 September 2026

The research library predates this recheck. Its existing files were reused; they were not downloaded again or presented as a new collection.

- All **149 local source files** still match their recorded SHA-256 hashes. The register has **164 entries**: 19 PDFs, 84 JPEG/PNG reference images, three SVG maps, 41 HTML snapshots and two CityGML files among the saved files. Seven video entries and six panorama entries retain their original viewer links. `validation.json` now reflects the complete register rather than the earlier 154-entry snapshot.
- The [official station website](https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/) and [floor guide](https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/floorguide/) remain accessible. Their current pages were checked; saved source snapshots retain their original retrieval timestamps.
- The [structural engineer's Kyoto Station project](https://www.kanebako-se.co.jp/works/view/240) remains accessible as a construction and structural detail reference.
- The [national architecture archive's Hara collection page](https://www.bunka.go.jp/nama/collection/hara/index.html?menu=3-0-10) confirms substantial Kyoto Station drawing and sketch holdings. Its counts of approximately 15,000 drawings and 4,000 sketches describe the entire Hara collection, not Kyoto Station alone.
- The linked [archive database](https://db.nama.bunka.go.jp/index.php/g476-hwyx-ws6b) still returned HTTP 403. Individual archival drawings were not obtained. A complete dimensioned as-built set remains unavailable in this library.

Video playback and interactive photosphere coverage have not been established by this check. The catalogue's inspection limits remain in force. Current floor maps can establish public-space relationships and tenancy; historical drawings and photographs require date reconciliation before using them to change the current station.
