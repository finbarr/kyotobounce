# Published canopy structure

Reference: *World Architecture* 61, November 1997, printed pp43–44 (PDF pp45–46), https://www.usmodernist.org/WORLD/WA-1997-61.pdf . Saved source: [complete PDF](../architecture/world-architecture-1997.pdf); [section preview](../architecture/world-architecture-045.png); [adjoining elevation/axonometric page](../architecture/world-architecture-046.png). Both complete pages and their drawings were visually inspected on 27 September 2026.

The structural account identifies a **2 m deep welded canopy truss** made from **100 mm square steel tubes with varying wall thicknesses**. This corroborates the existing layout's `trussDepthM: 2` and `squareTubeSideM: .1`. These dimensions are published nominal construction descriptions; member phase, wall thickness per member, brackets and exact end-joint geometry still require photographic registration. The new eastern and western outer-grid components do not yet include their complete inner trusses.

The account places the suspended public Skyway **45.2 m above the station entrance level**. It does not give the model's world datum or exact route endpoints. Do not set local Y to 45.2 or move a measured bridge endpoint solely from this sentence. The source model's 1.426 m spacing between two central shell surfaces is not the published structural depth.

The separate 13 m deep / 52 m span trusses described on printed p44 support the theatre and hotel above the subway. Those dimensions belong to the main building structure, not the glass canopy. The axonometric and longitudinal sections also corroborate the eastern courtyard's open relationship to the curved roof; the old flat roof/backing boxes were not evidence-backed representations of that space.

## Structural engineer corroboration

The [Kanebako project page](https://www.kanebako-se.co.jp/works/view/240), checked directly during the central hall study on 27 September 2026, explicitly gives the atrium basic grid as 1.44 m and its square steel tube width as 100 mm. The nominal pitch is now independently corroborated; phase, precise joints and wall thickness remain unresolved. Existing construction receipts retain their historical assumption wording.
