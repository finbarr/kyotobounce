## Observations

- The entrance photograph shows a dark granite surround, glazed double doors and a covered raised landing beside NIWA.
- Passage walls carry blue, red and yellow gallery booths above low rectangular lights. The ceiling has metal ribs and track lights; paving is dark square stone.
- The operator map confirms eastward access to lifts and toilets. It is a schematic, so dimensions and detailed facility plans are fitted.
