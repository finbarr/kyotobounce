# Basement 360° route inspection

Research update: 28 September 2026. This is a partial visual inspection of the operator-linked video, not a measured survey or a geometry change.

## Source and inspection scope

The [Kyoto Station Building directions page](https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/directions/) embeds [京都駅中央改札 → 地下鉄乗り場](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4q92JI4JhRM). The YouTube page identifies the uploader as **あるきナビ動画マップ**, marks the video unlisted and displays an approximate age of seven years. Capture date and exact publication date remain unverified. The player reports a duration of 57.861 seconds.

Playback was inspected at selected timestamps, including roughly 16, 26, 29.92, 30.46, 31.95, 33.95, 35, 39.92 and 45 seconds. At 29.92 and 31.95 seconds, the paused spherical view was dragged to inspect several directions. These are selected views, not complete continuous playback or a survey of every direction. No video file, sphere tiles or frame images were downloaded. Links below seek approximately; the viewer may open with a different orientation.

## What this resolves

The video independently supports the official illustrated route: first descent to B1F, turnback, then a second descent to B2F beneath the upper bank. It adds side views of the closed wall, service doors, adjoining corridor opening and overhead casing that a single forward photograph misses. See the [six-photo route analysis](BASEMENT-ROUTE-UPDATE.md).

The current assembly23 western return remains an **uninstalled provisional candidate**. This inspection does not validate its three-metre depth, opening width, shop bay widths, bank dimensions or absolute orientation. It also does not authorize a mirrored eastern return. Keep the existing closed-end evidence separate from the plan-derived western circulation connection.

## Remaining geometry questions

1. Register stable architectural anchors to identify the filmed bank and compass orientation before changing a particular end wall.
2. Recover the B2F floor elevation and lower-bank slope/run from defensible references. The accelerated route video cannot supply these through elapsed time or apparent pedestrian size.
3. Check the entire lower flight against the upper casing, B1F slab and B2F ceiling; preserve a real headroom envelope.
4. Match outer-corridor widths and portal positions to additional views. Current dimensions remain provisional.
5. Confirm current finishes following the 2025 B1F retail changes; the older footage supplies architectural context, not a current finish schedule.
