## Best starting points

The [official station website](https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/) and [floor guide](https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/floorguide/) establish public spaces and current facilities. The [JR West map page](https://eki.jr-odekake.net/premises?id=0610116) still identifies its diagram as dated 31 January 2026 and links an interactive 3D map. Both pages were checked during the 26–27 September research refresh.

The [city's Hachijo project page](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/tokei/page/0000301545.html) confirms the December 2016 forecourt renewal and links the completion brochure already in the library. Use it as a historical construction baseline and check current fixtures separately.

For architectural form, start with the saved World Architecture sections/elevations, FUJISASH curtain-wall drawings, operator event-space plans and structural-engineer photographs. This pass adds [a historical operator account](notes/URBAN-ADVANCE.md). No complete dimensioned as-built CAD/BIM set has been obtained.

## Recommended sequence and finish line

Register the reference views, prove one representative area, correct large architecture and routes, complete both approaches, then finish upper spaces and evening lighting. Require all 12 daylight and three evening comparisons to pass the shape, surface, light and completeness rubric.

Preserve gameplay, collision agreement and scoring. At the plan's fixed 1280x720/60 Hz Chrome configuration, target p95 frame intervals at or below 20 ms and p99 at or below 33.4 ms, repeated comparable trials, and a 20-minute session without sustained music slowdown or context loss. These are acceptance targets, not current performance claims.

Research files remain outside Git and the shipped asset pack. Source photographs are references; use original or suitably licensed game materials. The initial research pass collected evidence; subsequent local reconstruction work is recorded in the candidate notes. No reconstruction candidate has been deployed.
